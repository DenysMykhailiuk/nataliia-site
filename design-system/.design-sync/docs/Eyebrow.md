# Eyebrow

The small uppercase clay label above a heading: 0.8rem, letter-spaced 0.12em, 22px of space
below. Two or three words at most.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Перша зустріч</Eyebrow>
<h2>Як усе відбувається</h2>
```

On the dark testimonial band it lightens automatically — `SectionHead` and `QuoteWall`
place it for you.
